# Story of the World of Assassination Trilogy (spoilers)

Only reveal story details beyond the current game when the player asks.

## Main characters
- Agent 47: genetically engineered assassin working for the ICA
  (International Contract Agency). Wears a black suit and red tie.
- Diana Burnwood: 47's handler at the ICA. Calm, precise, dryly witty.
  Her parents were killed when she was a child, which ties into the story.
- Lucas Grey: a former child from the same facility as 47, where he was
  Subject 6 (revealed in a HITMAN 2 cutscene). Leads a militia against
  Providence. Known early on as the "Shadow Client".
- Providence: a secret organisation that controls governments and companies
  from the shadows. Led by three Partners and their operative, the Constant.
- Olivia Hall: a hacker working with Lucas Grey.

## HITMAN (2016)
47 joins the ICA after a training test (the Prologue). Missions in Paris,
Sapienza, Marrakesh, Bangkok, Colorado and Hokkaido. A mysterious Shadow
Client is killing members of Providence, using 47's ICA hits along the way.
Providence offers the ICA a deal to stop him.

## HITMAN 2
47 hunts the Shadow Client's militia leaders around the world. He learns the
Shadow Client is Lucas Grey and that both of them came from the same facility.
47 and Grey team up against Providence. The Constant is captured and reveals
the Partners' identities.

## HITMAN 3
47, Diana and Grey go after the three Partners: Carl Ingram and Marcus
Stuyvesant in Dubai, and Alexa Carlisle in Dartmoor. After Dartmoor the ICA
hunts them down. Grey shoots himself to distract the ICA agents and buy 47
time to escape. In Berlin the ICA sends agents after 47. In Chongqing 47 takes down the ICA itself. In Mendoza the
Constant, Arthur Edwards, gets involved, and the trilogy ends in the
Carpathian Mountains aboard Edwards' train (Untouchable).
