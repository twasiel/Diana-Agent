# Ratings and Classic Challenges

These apply to the World of Assassination trilogy (HITMAN 2016, HITMAN 2, HITMAN 3).
Players often abbreviate them: SA, SO, SASO.

## Silent Assassin (SA)
The highest mission rating, and a challenge on every location.
It is NOT a nickname for Agent 47. To earn it, ALL of these must be true:
- Only the targets die. Any non-target death breaks it, including accidents.
- No bodies found. If any body, dead or unconscious, is discovered, it breaks.
  Exception: deaths that look like accidents (falls, electrocution, drowning,
  explosions staged as accidents, some poisons) do not break it when found.
- Never spotted. No one may see 47 commit a crime or recognise him as a threat.
  Combat breaks it. Even the target seeing 47 counts, killing them afterwards
  does not undo it.
- No recordings. If a security camera records 47, the rating is lost unless
  he destroys or erases the recorder/evidence before leaving the mission.
Knocking out non-targets is allowed, as long as their bodies are hidden
(closets, containers) and never found.
Tip: Options > Gameplay > HUD has a "Silent Assassin HUD" that shows two pistol
icons, green while the rating is intact, red once it is broken.
Note: some details (for example how knocked-out or sedated targets are treated)
have changed between game updates.

## Suit Only (SO)
Complete the mission without ever changing out of your starting suit.
No disguises at any point. 47 may still subdue people and use any items.

## Silent Assassin, Suit Only (SASO)
Both at once: every Silent Assassin condition AND never change out of the suit.
Usually the hardest classic challenge on a location. Common approaches: pick a
starting location and agency pickup that put 47 close to the targets, smuggle
tools in through the loadout, and use accident kills or poison so found bodies
don't matter.

## Sniper Assassin
Eliminate all targets with a sniper rifle, while also keeping Silent Assassin.
Rifles are usually smuggled in inside the ICA briefcase via the loadout or an
agency pickup, or taken from a guard on site. Gunshots alert guards to the area,
but that alone does not count as being spotted if 47 leaves before they arrive.

## Classic challenges in general
Each location has a "Classics" category containing Silent Assassin, Suit Only,
Silent Assassin Suit Only and Sniper Assassin. Completing them gives mastery XP.
Other challenge categories include Assassinations (specific kill methods),
Discovery (exploring the map), Feats and Targets.

## Default Loadout (DL)
Complete a mission using only the default loadout, that means only an silenced 
pistol (ICA-19), a fiber wire, and 3 coins as distractions.
