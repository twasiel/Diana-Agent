# Gameplay Mechanics

## Disguises
Every outfit decides where 47 can legally go and who treats him with suspicion.
Taking an NPC's clothes (after subduing or killing them) gives 47 their
disguise. Many maps have unique disguises tied to a Mission Story.

## Enforcers
NPCs who can see through the disguise 47 is wearing, for example a head chef
noticing a fake cook. They show a white dot above their head. Stay out of their
sight or keep moving away when their suspicion builds.

## Blend-in spots
Places where 47 can perform an activity that fits his disguise (sweeping,
using a laptop, playing an instrument). While blending in he is much harder
for enforcers to see through.

## Instinct
The vision mode. Highlights targets and mission-critical objects in red and
shows NPCs through walls as outlines. It no longer hides 47 from enforcers
(unlike Hitman: Absolution) and can be used freely or turned off in settings.

## Suspicion, trespassing and hostile areas
Trespassing areas: 47 is told to leave if caught. Hostile areas: guards attack
or arrest him. Illegal actions (carrying weapons openly, picking locks,
subduing) raise suspicion when seen.

## Accidents
Kills that look accidental: pushing someone off a ledge, dropping a chandelier,
electrocution, explosions, drowning in a toilet or fountain, poisoned food or
drink with a lethal poison. Bodies from accidents don't break Silent Assassin.

## Subduing and hiding bodies
47 can knock people out from behind, with a blunt item, or with sedative
poison. Hide bodies in containers, closets or bins so they are never found.

## Security cameras
Cameras record 47 committing illegal acts or being somewhere he shouldn't.
Destroy or erase the recorder (usually in a security room) to remove the
evidence. Shooting the recorder also works.

## Common tools
- Fiber Wire: silent strangling, 47's signature weapon.
- Coins: throw to distract NPCs and move them.
- Lockpick, crowbar, screwdriver, wrench: open doors and sabotage objects.
- Emetic poison makes the victim run to a toilet or sink to vomit, isolating
  them. Sedative poison knocks out. Lethal poison kills (counts as an accident).
- Remote explosives and proximity mines: also used for accident-style kills.
- ICA Briefcase: smuggles a large item, like a sniper rifle, through checks.

## Loadout, starting locations and agency pickups
Before a mission 47 chooses a suit, gear, a starting location and agency
pickups (hidden stashes of items placed in the level). More starting locations
and pickups unlock as Mastery level goes up.

## Difficulty
- Casual: unlimited saves, Mission Story guidance, no security cameras,
  fewer enforcers, NPCs less attentive.
- Professional: the standard, unlimited saves, cameras present.
- Master: one save per mission, no mission guidance, more cameras and guards,
  NPCs more sensitive to sounds, bloody kills ruin the disguise.
In HITMAN 3 / World of Assassination every difficulty is available from the
start. In the original HITMAN (2016), Master had to be unlocked first.
