# Game Modes and Progression

## Story missions
The main mission at each location (see the mission and target tools for names,
targets and the achievement for completing each one).

## Mission Stories (called "Opportunities" in HITMAN 2016)
Guided chains of events inside a mission that reveal unusual ways to kill
targets, for example posing as a famous person or a surgeon. Optional;
on Master difficulty the guidance is turned off.

## Mastery
Each location has its own Mastery level, raised by XP from challenges and
completed missions. Most locations go up to level 20, some smaller ones less.
Levels unlock starting locations, agency pickups, weapons, tools and suits.
Unlocked items can be used on any location.

## Escalation Contracts
Short series of levels on one location, usually three to five. Every level
repeats the hit with new targets, complications or restrictions (for example
more cameras or a required kill method). Completing all levels gives a reward,
often a suit or item.

## Elusive Targets
Limited-time targets that are available for a short window only. They are
one-shot: if the target escapes or 47 dies, the attempt is over and can't be
retried. Completing them usually rewards a suit. Some return later in
re-releases.

## Elusive Target Arcade
A permanent mode with a series of past Elusive Targets in a row, each with an
optional complication. Failing a stage restarts from the first stage and locks
the contract for a while.

## Contracts Mode
Players create their own contracts: mark any NPC as a target, set the kill
method and conditions, and share it. Featured Contracts are hand-picked ones
highlighted by IO Interactive.

## Freelancer
A roguelike mode added to World of Assassination in January 2023. 47 operates
from his own Safehouse and takes down criminal syndicates across campaigns.
Each campaign has several missions where he hunts syndicate members, ending
in a Showdown where the syndicate leader hides among suspects and must be
identified from clues (outfit, habits, guards). 47 earns Merces, the mode's
currency, from objectives and spends them on gear at suppliers. Failing a
mission loses the gear he brought and some Merces.

## Sniper Assassin (mode)
A separate mode from HITMAN 2 where 47 stays in one position with a sniper
rifle and eliminates targets from range. Not the same as the "Sniper Assassin"
classic challenge in normal missions.

## Bonus content
- Patient Zero: a bonus campaign from HITMAN Game of the Year Edition, about
  a virus outbreak.
- The Sarajevo Six: six bonus contracts telling a side-story about former
  members of the CICADA paramilitary unit.
